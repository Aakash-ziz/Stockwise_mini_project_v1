# StockWise — Entity Analysis

This document identifies, classifies, and documents the business entities that comprise the StockWise application. It defines entity boundaries, lifecycles, dependencies, events, and business rules conceptually to establish the blueprints for subsequent database modeling, without introducing database tables, schemas, columns, attributes, primary/foreign keys, cardinality, normalization, SQL, or ER diagrams.

---

## 1. Complete Entity Inventory & Priority

### Core Entities (Required for MVP)
1. **Product**: Master catalog profiles of retail items. (Priority: Core | Business Importance: Critical)
2. **Category**: Organizational groupings for catalog classification. (Priority: Core | Business Importance: High)
3. **Inventory**: Real-time stock counts and threshold indicators for products. (Priority: Core | Business Importance: Critical)
4. **Sale**: Records of completed customer checkouts. (Priority: Core | Business Importance: Critical)
5. **Sale Item**: Detailed items and quantities associated with a sale. (Priority: Core | Business Importance: Critical)
6. **AI Recommendation**: Advisory alerts suggesting replenishment or audit actions. (Priority: Core | Business Importance: High)
7. **Audit Log**: Permanent logs tracking adjustments and price history. (Priority: Core | Business Importance: Critical)

### Business Support Entities (Needed Soon)
8. **User**: System operator credentials and profile. (Priority: Supporting | Business Importance: High)
9. **Role**: Groupings of system operator roles (e.g. Sales, Admin). (Priority: Supporting | Business Importance: High)
10. **Permission**: Action rights granted to roles. (Priority: Supporting | Business Importance: High)
11. **Supplier**: Distributor entities supplying inventory. (Priority: Supporting | Business Importance: Medium)
12. **Purchase**: Logs of procurement orders sent to suppliers. (Priority: Supporting | Business Importance: High)
13. **Purchase Item**: Individual lines of products inside a purchase order. (Priority: Supporting | Business Importance: High)
14. **Customer**: Records of customers associated with transactions. (Priority: Supporting | Business Importance: Medium)
15. **Warehouse**: Storage facility locations holding inventory. (Priority: Supporting | Business Importance: Low for MVP, High for scaling)
16. **Stock Adjustment**: Records of manual stock balance corrections. (Priority: Supporting | Business Importance: High)
17. **Notification**: User-facing system alert dispatch logs. (Priority: Supporting | Business Importance: Medium)

### Future Entities (Future Roadmap)

## 2. Detailed Entity Profiles

For each entity, we define classification, conceptual lifecycle, business ownership, boundaries, dependency analysis, lifecycle events, business rules, and expandability.

### 2.1 Product
* **Classification**: Master Entity
* **Entity Boundary**: Stores only the basic static description, base cost parameters, and metadata identifying a catalog item. It does not track active counts or stock levels directly.
* **Logical Dependencies**: Product conceptually belongs to a Category. It is referenced by Inventory, Sale Items, and Purchase Items.
* **Business Ownership**: 
  * *Primary Owner*: Store Owner
  * *Creator*: Administrator, Inventory Manager
  * *Editor*: Administrator, Inventory Manager
  * *Viewer*: All Actors (Admin, Manager, Sales, Owner, Future Customer)
* **Conceptual Lifecycle**: `Created` ➔ `Active` ➔ `Inactive` ➔ `Retired`
* **Lifecycle Events**:
  * *Product Created*: Profile created with name, unique SKU, prices, and category.
  * *Product Updated*: Profile metadata, description, or pricing is modified.
  * *Product Activated*: Made eligible for transaction checkout.
  * *Product Deactivated*: Suspended from active checkouts (e.g., seasonal pause).
  * *Product Retired*: Discontinued. Kept for historical reference but excluded from active sales.
* **Business Rules**:
  * SKU must be unique across the entire system.
  * Product must be assigned to an active Category.
  * Purchase price and selling price must be non-negative numbers.
  * Product cannot be hard-deleted if it has historical sales transactions associated with it.
* **Future Expandability**: Support for multiple product images, barcode scanning tags, variant sizes/colors, and batch/expiration date tracking.

### 2.2 Category
* **Classification**: Reference Entity
* **Entity Boundary**: Manages organizational taxonomy for products. It holds category names and descriptions. It does not store product details.
* **Logical Dependencies**: Independent reference parent entity. Products depend on Categories.
* **Business Ownership**:
  * *Primary Owner*: Store Owner / Administrator
  * *Creator*: Administrator
  * *Editor*: Administrator
  * *Viewer*: All Actors
* **Conceptual Lifecycle**: `Created` ➔ `Active` ➔ `Archived`
* **Lifecycle Events**:
  * *Category Created*: Taxonomy group defined.
  * *Category Updated*: Category name or description modified.
  * *Category Archived*: Deprecated group. Associated products must be reassigned.
* **Business Rules**:
  * Category name must be unique.
  * Cannot delete or archive a category if it still has active products assigned to it.
* **Future Expandability**: Support for hierarchical nested subcategories (parent-child relationships).

### 2.3 Inventory
* **Classification**: Master Entity
* **Entity Boundary**: Tracks the active quantity, threshold targets (minimum stock level), and status of a physical item. It does not store general product descriptors like name or category.
* **Logical Dependencies**: Directly depends on Product.
* **Business Ownership**:
  * *Primary Owner*: Inventory Manager
  * *Creator*: Auto-created upon Product Creation
  * *Editor*: System (sales/replenishments), Inventory Manager & Admin (manual adjustments)
  * *Viewer*: All Actors
* **Conceptual Lifecycle**: `Created` ➔ `Updated` ➔ `Adjusted` ➔ `Archived` (when Product is retired)
* **Lifecycle Events**:
  * *Stock Received*: Inbound supplier stock added to the balance.
  * *Stock Deducted*: Outbound stock subtracted automatically due to sales.
  * *Stock Adjusted*: Manual balance overrides logged (e.g., audits, damages).
  * *Threshold Tripped*: Stock falls below minimum, triggering `LOW STOCK` or `OUT OF STOCK` status.
* **Business Rules**:
  * Stock quantity cannot be negative.
  * Every inventory entry must correspond to exactly one Product.
  * Status (In Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock) must dynamically reflect the relationship between quantity and min-stock threshold.
* **Future Expandability**: Support for multi-warehouse tracking, batch/lot management, and precise location coordinates (aisle/bin/shelf).

### 2.4 Sale
* **Classification**: Transaction Entity
* **Entity Boundary**: Captures the overall checkout transaction details, including time, date, customer reference, discount, payment summary, and total revenue. It does not store itemized details.
* **Logical Dependencies**: Depends on Customer (if registered) and checkout User. Parent of Sale Item.
* **Business Ownership**:
  * *Primary Owner*: Store Owner / Finance Auditor
  * *Creator*: Sales Staff, Administrator
  * *Editor*: Immutable once completed
  * *Viewer*: Administrator, Store Owner, Inventory Manager
* **Conceptual Lifecycle**: `Created` ➔ `Completed` ➔ `Archived` (for audit retention)
* **Lifecycle Events**:
  * *Sale Drafted*: Customer checkout initiated.
  * *Sale Completed*: Checkout payment finalized, inventory deduction triggered.
  * *Sale Cancelled*: Unresolved checkout deleted before completion.
* **Business Rules**:
  * Completed sales are immutable. Any correction requires a separate refund transaction.
  * Every sale must be assigned a unique transaction identifier.
  * Transaction timestamp must represent the actual checkout finalization time.
* **Future Expandability**: Multi-channel sales separation (in-store POS vs. online customer orders), tracking payment types (Cash, Card, Digital), and tax computations.

### 2.5 Sale Item
* **Classification**: Transaction Entity
* **Entity Boundary**: Manages line-item details of a sale, tracking which product was sold, what quantity was purchased, and the unit price at checkout.
* **Logical Dependencies**: Depends entirely on Sale and Product.
* **Business Ownership**: Same as Sale (immutable once Sale is completed).
* **Conceptual Lifecycle**: `Created` ➔ `Completed` ➔ `Archived`
* **Lifecycle Events**:
  * *Line Item Added*: Product and quantity registered to a checkout cart.
  * *Line Item Finalized*: Unit price locked and checkout completed.
* **Business Rules**:
  * Sold quantity must be a positive whole number greater than zero.
  * Selling price recorded must match the active product price at checkout (unless discount is applied).
  * Cannot exceed available inventory quantity.
* **Future Expandability**: Support for product variant mappings and return status indicators per line item.

### 2.6 AI Recommendation
* **Classification**: System Entity
* **Entity Boundary**: Stores advisory insights generated by system analysis, such as suggested reorders, liquidation warnings, or audit prompts.
* **Logical Dependencies**: Depends on Inventory levels and historical Sales records.
* **Business Ownership**:
  * *Primary Owner*: Inventory Manager
  * *Creator*: System algorithms
  * *Editor*: Inventory Manager, Administrator (to change status)
  * *Viewer*: Administrator, Inventory Manager, Store Owner
* **Conceptual Lifecycle**: `Generated` ➔ `Pending Action` ➔ `Approved` OR `Dismissed`
* **Lifecycle Events**:
  * *Recommendation Generated*: Alert created based on stock and velocity analysis.
  * *Recommendation Approved*: Manager approves reorder, spawning draft purchase order.
  * *Recommendation Dismissed*: Manager rejects recommendation with reason.
* **Business Rules**:
  * Must specify target product, suggested quantity, and action type.
  * Automatically updates to "Expired" if inventory is replenished through other actions.
* **Future Expandability**: Feedback loops where predictive models adjust recommendations based on historical manager acceptance rates.

### 2.7 Audit Log
* **Classification**: System Entity
* **Entity Boundary**: Captures historical event details (such as adjustments, price changes, or deletions) to maintain accountability. It is an append-only registry.
* **Logical Dependencies**: Depends on the event trigger, initiating User, and affected entity.
* **Business Ownership**:
  * *Primary Owner*: Store Owner / Compliance Auditor
  * *Creator*: System triggers (automated)
  * *Editor*: Immutable (no editing or deletion allowed)
  * *Viewer*: Administrator, Store Owner
* **Conceptual Lifecycle**: `Recorded` ➔ `Archived`
* **Lifecycle Events**:
  * *Log Recorded*: Append-only entry saved upon a auditable business action.
* **Business Rules**:
  * Audit logs must be completely immutable. They cannot be modified, deleted, or re-ordered.
  * Every entry must record timestamp, author identity, affected entity, action type, and pre/post values.
* **Future Expandability**: Security alerts (flagging anomalous repeated price overrides or negative stock adjustments).

## 3. Business Support Entities

### 3.1 User
* **Classification**: Master Entity
* **Boundary**: Stores staff credentials, names, and contact details.
* **Dependencies**: Associated with a Role.
* **Ownership**: Admin (Owner), Owner (Viewer).
* **Lifecycle**: `Created` ➔ `Active` ➔ `Suspended` ➔ `Archived`
* **Events**: Created, Password Reset, Suspended, Reactivated.
* **Business Rules**: Unique email/username identifier.

### 3.2 Role
* **Classification**: Reference Entity
* **Boundary**: Groups permission rights (e.g. Sales, Admin, Manager).
* **Dependencies**: Independent, referenced by User and Permissions.
* **Ownership**: Admin.
* **Lifecycle**: `Created` ➔ `Active` ➔ `Archived`
* **Events**: Role Created, Permissions Bound.
* **Business Rules**: Unique name. Role cannot be deleted if users are assigned.

### 3.3 Permission
* **Classification**: Reference Entity
* **Boundary**: Stores conceptual operations (e.g., `edit_price`, `adjust_stock`).
* **Dependencies**: Linked to Roles.
* **Ownership**: System/Admin.
* **Lifecycle**: `System Defined` (static).
* **Business Rules**: Uniquely identified by system keys.

### 3.4 Supplier
* **Classification**: Master Entity
* **Boundary**: Stores supplier company name, contact, and lead time configurations.
* **Dependencies**: Independent. Referenced by Products and Purchases.
* **Ownership**: Inventory Manager.
* **Lifecycle**: `Created` ➔ `Active` ➔ `Inactive`
* **Events**: Registered, Supplier Profile Updated, Status Suspended.
* **Business Rules**: Company name must be unique.

### 3.5 Purchase (Purchase Order)
* **Classification**: Transaction Entity
* **Boundary**: Tracks outbound replenishment orders sent to suppliers.
* **Dependencies**: Depends on Supplier. Parent of Purchase Items.
* **Ownership**: Inventory Manager.
* **Lifecycle**: `Drafted` ➔ `Sent` ➔ `Partially Received` ➔ `Completed` ➔ `Cancelled`
* **Events**: Order Created, Dispatched, Arrived, Stock Logged.
* **Business Rules**: Cannot receive stock without a referencing Purchase Order status change.

### 3.6 Purchase Item
* **Classification**: Transaction Entity
* **Boundary**: Holds line-item quantities and contract costs of products in a purchase order.
* **Dependencies**: Linked to Purchase and Product.
* **Ownership**: Inventory Manager.
* **Lifecycle**: `Created` ➔ `Received`
* **Business Rules**: Quantities must be positive numbers.

### 3.7 Customer
* **Classification**: Master Entity
* **Boundary**: Customer records (name, email, phone) to link sales transactions.
* **Dependencies**: Referenced by Sale.
* **Ownership**: Store Owner.
* **Lifecycle**: `Created` ➔ `Active` ➔ `Archived`
* **Events**: Registered, Profile Updated.
* **Business Rules**: Email address must be unique if provided.

### 3.8 Warehouse
* **Classification**: Reference Entity
* **Boundary**: Stores physical storage facilities.
* **Dependencies**: Referenced by Inventory and Stock Movements.
* **Ownership**: Administrator.
* **Lifecycle**: `Created` ➔ `Active` ➔ `Archived`
* **Events**: Warehouse Registered, Closed.
* **Business Rules**: Warehouse identifier must be unique.

### 3.9 Stock Adjustment
* **Classification**: Transaction Entity
* **Boundary**: Logs manual stock overrides (quantity changed, reason, author).
* **Dependencies**: Depends on Product, Inventory, and User.
* **Ownership**: Inventory Manager.
* **Lifecycle**: `Recorded` (Immutable)
* **Events**: Logged.
* **Business Rules**: Must specify a valid reason (e.g., Audit Discrepancy, Damage, Theft).

### 3.10 Notification
* **Classification**: System Entity
* **Boundary**: System notifications sent to users (low stock alerts, sales summaries).
* **Dependencies**: Associated with a User or Role.
* **Ownership**: System / User.
* **Lifecycle**: `Unread` ➔ `Read` ➔ `Archived`
* **Events**: Dispatched, Read.

## 4. Entity Interaction Summary

The following summary outlines how entities participate in key business processes and which actors interact with them:

* **Product Registration**: Uses **Product**, **Category**, and **Supplier** entities. Interacted with by the *Administrator* and *Inventory Manager*.
* **Inventory Replenishment**: Uses **Purchase**, **Purchase Item**, **Product**, **Inventory**, and **Supplier** entities. Interacted with by the *Inventory Manager* and *Supplier*.
* **Sales Processing**: Uses **Sale**, **Sale Item**, **Product**, **Inventory**, and **Customer** entities. Interacted with by the *Sales Staff* and *Customer*.
* **Stock Adjustment**: Uses **Inventory**, **Product**, **Stock Adjustment**, and **Audit Log** entities. Interacted with by the *Inventory Manager* and *Administrator*.
* **AI Recommendation Generation**: Uses **AI Recommendation**, **Inventory**, and **Sale** entities. Generated by the *System* and reviewed by the *Inventory Manager* and *Administrator*.
* **Auditing**: Uses **Audit Log**, **User**, **Product**, and **Stock Adjustment** entities. Reviewed by the *Store Owner* and *Administrator*.
